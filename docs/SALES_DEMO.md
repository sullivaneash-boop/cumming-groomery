# Walkthrough for Shelby

## Start on a phone

Open Home at a phone width. Let Shelby see the real salon photo and the two ways to act immediately. Then open Services, tap Gallery and enlarge a photo. Finish on Visit by showing the address, directions and one-tap call.

## Show what works today

The menu, gallery filters, image viewer, frequently asked questions and contact links work. Book Appointment opens the salon's current Pawfinity page. Show the New Client Application and returning-client entry points without filling in the form. Explain that the salon keeps handling appointments in the same system.

## Explain the example content

The demo uses supplied photos from the salon's Facebook materials. Full grooming, bath and brush, nail care and add-ons illustrate how an approved menu might read. Customer Stories displays three supplied Google Maps reviews, and the staff section has space for an approved introduction. The Peakline Media concept label identifies the independent demonstration.

## Ask for the launch details

Confirm services and pricing language, hours, the preferred booking instructions and contact details. Ask which photos, team details and reviews Shelby wants to share, and confirm usage rights and official profile links. Confirm scope, pricing and launch ownership before making the site public.

## Keep the offer simple

> This gives your groomery a place customers can find, see your work and know how to reach you. The appointment button takes them to the system you already use.

Start with the five-page website. When useful, add a simple photo editor, a seasonal announcement, an approved gift-card link or a new-client page. An email form can be evaluated if customers have a clear need for it.
