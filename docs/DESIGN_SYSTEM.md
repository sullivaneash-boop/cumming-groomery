# Design system

The leading reference is Chilltown Pet Spa's independent, pet-first grooming site. Carry over its clear species language, prominent booking and telephone choices, and substantial animal photography. The concept's art direction comes from the supplied three-dog mark and the brief's neighborhood boutique positioning. Use a dark forest-green editorial hero beside a close portrait on the actual salon grooming table, followed by spacious ivory sections. [Chilltown Pet Spa](https://www.chilltownpetspa.com/)

- Borrow category scanability from Dogtopia and the pairing of visit logistics with contact from Modern Animal. Give the existing booking destination an action style as distinct as the primary action in GlossGenius. [Lazyweb references](https://www.lazyweb.com/agentic-search/ae60978e-c925-466f-893c-195830ffbaa0)
- Reserve clay for appointment actions and selected accents. Use forest for the main hero, footer and text, sage for quiet section backgrounds, and ivory for reading surfaces. The CSS variables in `app/globals.css` are authoritative.
- Use Playfair Display for display headings, DM Sans for paragraphs, controls and navigation. The hero uses an italic second line as the full second beat of the headline.
- Use crisp image edges, delicate borders, limited shadows and broad section spacing. Keep service titles directly below images and use square-cornered controls with a visible focus ring.
- Use one dominant image above the fold. Let the gallery show a mix of real dog and cat photos, plus a look inside the salon. Identify salon photos near prominent media and reserve a separate data category for any future illustrative images.
- Body copy defaults to 16px or larger. Primary controls have at least 44px height. Use native details and dialog, keyboard-accessible links, visible focus and reduced-motion support.
